- Two long pale-green translucent ribbon twin-tails hang from the round hair ornaments on both sides of the head down to her hips, ending in clover charms.
- A small black rectangular hair clip with a green symbol is pinned in the FRONT bangs on her LEFT side. It is never on the back of the head, so it is not visible in back or three-quarter back views.
