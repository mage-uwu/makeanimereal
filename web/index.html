    Each frame
    1. Rank the four key drawings around the camera by angle. The nearest one is the drawing; it hands over halfway to the next key.
    2. Project that drawing onto the hidden mesh from the exact camera it was drawn for, then view the mesh from the current camera. The drawing bends with her 3D shape. Each drawing was fitted to the mesh beforehand, so its eyes, bow and hems sit on the mesh's.
    3. Where its camera could not see a surface, or saw it edge-on, take that surface whole from the next drawing that did. Pixels are never mixed, and the mesh's own colours never show.
    4. Paint art that sits off the mesh, like the ribbons, from the same drawing onto a card behind her.
