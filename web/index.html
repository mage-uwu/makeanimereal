    Neuromation
    Latent rotation. Every drawing is a view of one latent object, and each was drawn from a slightly different vantage than requested (often turned a few degrees less, or seen from a little higher). That vantage is learned per drawing, and the drawing becomes a rigid shell: every point sits on the ray from its vantage at a smoothed depth, so the vantage sees it exactly as drawn and the shell can't shear or tear. Between keys the shell rotates; your angle maps to a latent angle that blends the nearby drawings' vantages. Depth relief sets how curved the shell is, from a flat card (no deformation at all) to the full smoothed shape.
